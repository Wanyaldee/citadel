## About the removed upstream legal documents

Earlier versions of this repository (inherited from upstream Zed) included `terms.md`, `privacy-policy.md`, `subprocessors.md`, and `third-party-terms.md` describing Zed Industries' hosted SaaS product (collaboration servers, paid plans, subprocessor list, etc.). Those documents described a commercial service operated by Zed Industries, Inc. that Citadel does not offer, so they were removed rather than kept and renamed — retaining them under a "Citadel" label would have misrepresented terms that belong to a different company and a different product. This project does not operate any hosted service, does not collect telemetry beyond what upstream Zed's client already implements, and has no separate terms of service, privacy policy, or subprocessor list of its own at this time.

If that changes, this file will be updated accordingly.
